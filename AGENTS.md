# Rana design rule

Preserve the user-approved typography. Do not introduce or restore black text strokes, hard outlines around letters, or equivalent stacked hard text/drop shadows.

Jarrett’s explicit exception is legal liability regarding accessibility. Apply that exception only when a specific, documented accessibility-related legal obligation makes the treatment necessary. A general contrast concern, an optional WCAG technique, or a design preference is not sufficient justification.

Use compatible alternatives for contrast when needed. Ordinary keyboard focus indicators, control borders, and the already-approved soft text shadows are not text strokes and should remain where useful.

This instruction was explicitly requested on 2026-09-05 after the hard text outlines introduced in accessibility commit `9c8afed` were rejected.
