Ready Now Made To Order Consultation FAQ

Current boundaries.

A readable summary so you can decide how to continue. It is not legal boilerplate, and it does not replace the live site.

Live-site terms govern any live order. Purchases, consultations, shipping, and returns happen under Rana’s current public terms at the time of the order — not under this page.

Custom and made-to-order

Custom and made-to-order work is final sale. It is not treated as ordinary ready inventory.

Eligible returns

Where a return is eligible under live terms, it currently carries a 10% restocking fee and must be shipped back within 72 hours. Always confirm the live terms before you buy.

Loss, theft, and later repair

For six months after custom delivery, Rana addresses problems with her work itself. Loss and theft are outside that boundary. Repairs after that window are considered case by case. This is not insurance, and it is not a lifetime guarantee.

Source and collaborator boundary

Stones and settings may come from different hands. Natural-gem collaborator work is purchased through the collaborator’s own shop; settings may continue through Rana. See collaborators and how Rana works.

What this site is

This site is a place to look and decide. It is not a store, scheduler, account system, or contract platform. External links leave for Rana’s live surfaces, where purchases and live terms live.

Live terms: ranalevyjewelry.com/services and related pages on the live site. If anything here disagrees with the live site, the live site wins.
